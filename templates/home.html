Rules & Guidelines

Read through the platform instructions carefully before starting any exam.

Strict Code of Conduct

  • Stable Network: Ensure a steady internet connection. The timer does not stop if you drop offline.
  • Fullscreen Lock: All assessments require fullscreen. Exiting fullscreen logs a warning in the database.
  • Tab Switch Detection: Navigating away triggers warnings. Exceeding 3 warnings submits the exam.
  • Disablers: Copy/paste, keyboard shortcuts, and right-clicks are strictly disabled inside the exam room.
